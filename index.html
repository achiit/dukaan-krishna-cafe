Our story

A small corner of Chirahula Colony that feels like home.

My uncle used to run a small tea stall, and I grew up watching how a ₹10 cup of chai could start a conversation between strangers. That memory is the reason this cafe exists.

We opened Krishna Cafe so the colony would have a clean, warm place to sit after college, after work, or after a long day. The lights glow late into the night and the outdoor seats fill up fast on festival evenings.

Come as you are. Order a chai or a full meal, either way you are welcome at our table.
